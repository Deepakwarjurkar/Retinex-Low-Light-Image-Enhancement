How Retinex works

Image formation based on illumination and reflectance.

I

Observed Image

Captured low-light image.

→
L

Illumination

Estimated lighting component.

+
R

Reflectance

Scene information independent of illumination.

→
✦

Enhanced Image

Improved visibility and contrast.

RETINEX MODEL
I(x,y) = R(x,y) × L(x,y)
SSR
SSR = log(I) − log(Gσ * I)
MSR
MSR = Σ wᵢ SSRᵢ
SSR

Single-Scale Retinex

Uses a single Gaussian scale to estimate surrounding illumination and enhance local image structure.

MSR

Multi-Scale Retinex

Combines responses from multiple Gaussian scales for more balanced illumination enhancement.
